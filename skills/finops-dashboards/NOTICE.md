This skill restates facts and ideas from the works below in its own words. No text or code was copied. Each work keeps its own licence; none of the authors or projects endorses this skill.

## Specifications and frameworks

- FinOps Foundation, FinOps Framework (phases, capabilities, maturity model). <https://www.finops.org/framework/>
- FinOps Foundation, FOCUS specification v1.4 (cost column definitions). <https://focus.finops.org/>
- W3C, WCAG 2.2 Understanding documents for SC 1.4.1 and 1.4.11 (W3C Document License). <https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/>
- NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook of Statistical Methods, section 1.3.5.17 (US government work). <https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/>
- International Business Communication Standards, public summary of the notation rules. <https://www.ibcs.com/>
- Council Directive 2006/112/EC (VAT), articles 44 and 196.

- Hetzner Online documentation and Cloud API specification (billing rules, pricing endpoint). <https://docs.hetzner.com> <https://docs.hetzner.cloud>
- AWS, Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud documentation of their cost anomaly detection and amortization.
- CBS and Rijksoverheid figures for Dutch electricity tariffs and energy tax; business.gov.nl for Dutch VAT rates.
