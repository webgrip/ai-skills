# YouTube packaging: evidence

Labels: **OFF** official YouTube Help or staff · **DATA** large-sample, correlational · **VENDOR**
vendor blog, method not published · **ANEC** practitioner convention · **GAP** not verified.
Official pages change; re-read them before relying on a limit.

## Thumbnails

- OFF: 16:9, JPG/PNG/GIF, 2 MB limit for mobile upload, 1280×720 the working size, custom
  thumbnails need a verified account; Shorts covers are 9:16 and set on desktop
  ([72431](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431)).
- OFF: Test & Compare shows thumbnails below 1280×720 at reduced quality for the whole test
  ([13861714](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13861714)).
- DATA: faces appear in most breakout thumbnails and equally often in underperformers (Eden, 507k
  videos); 1of10 finds faces and no faces about equal. A face is optional.
  ([Eden](https://eden.so/blog/what-makes-a-good-youtube-thumbnail/),
  [summary](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/do-faces-help-youtube-thumbnails-heres-what-the-data-says/563944/))
- DATA: most breakouts carry text, median five words (vidIQ); Eden puts the range at four to six.
- DATA: brightness, saturation and contrast did not separate winners from losers (Eden).
- ANEC: one focal point, text clear of the duration badge, title and thumbnail as two halves of
  one promise.

## Test & Compare (OFF, 13861714)

Full evidence, sample sizes and the test log: [testing.md](testing.md).

Up to three thumbnails and/or titles, desktop only; the winner is chosen by watch time share;
usually done within two weeks; outcomes Winner, Performed the same, Inconclusive; editing the title
or thumbnail stops it; not available for Shorts, private, made-for-kids or age-restricted videos;
low-impression videos rarely produce a winner.

## Titles and metadata

- OFF: titles up to 100 characters. ANEC: about 60 show before truncation.
- ANEC: technical buyers reward clarity over curiosity bait; no controlled study found.
- OFF chapters: first timestamp `00:00`, at least three, each at least 10 s
  ([9884579](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9884579)).
- OFF hashtags: up to three shown above the title; more than 60 and all are ignored
  ([6390658](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658)).
- OFF end screens: last 5 to 20 s, video at least 25 s, up to four elements; external links need
  the Partner Program ([6388789](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6388789)).
- OFF tags: "tags play a minimal role in your video's discovery"; useful for common misspellings;
  title, thumbnail and description matter more ([146402](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/146402)).
- GAP: description limit 5000 characters and tags 500 characters, as the Studio form enforces;
  the help pages were not re-read.
- OFF paid promotion: disclose "branded content, sponsorships, endorsements, or other commercial
  relationships" ([154235](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/154235)). The page does not
  say whether your own product counts; reading it as a third-party relationship is ANEC.

## Disclosure (OFF, [14328491](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491))

Disclose realistic content that shows a real person saying or doing something they did not,
alters footage of a real event or place, or invents a realistic scene. Not required for animated
or clearly unrealistic content, help with scripts or captions, or cloning your own voice. A generic
synthetic narrator over animation is not one of the listed cases.

## Shorts

- OFF: up to three minutes ([10059070](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10059070)); since
  31 March 2025 every start or replay counts as a view and "engaged views" is the former metric.
- OFF, via interview (YouTube's Shorts product lead, 2023): "viewed vs swiped away" exists, its
  thresholds are not public, storytelling beats a target length, early views are exploratory
  ([report](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-explains-how-shorts-algorithm-works/494953/)).
- OFF: a Short can link one related video ([14075157](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14075157)).
- VENDOR: overlay safe zone of about 300 to 400 px at the bottom, 120 to 290 px at the top and
  100 to 200 px on the right of 1080×1920.
- VENDOR: hook-retention and ideal-length benchmarks conflict; compare against your own Shorts.
- GAP: loops ranking higher; a 70 to 90 % viewed-vs-swiped target attributed to one analyst.

## Drafts and licensing

- OFF: videos are available for Shorts remixing by default; the Shorts remixing setting sits under
  Show more; blocking audio-only remixing needs Content Manager
  ([10623810](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10623810)).

- OFF: unlisted videos are link-only with comments; private videos are shared by email without
  comments ([157177](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/157177)).
- OFF (ElevenLabs docs): paid plans grant commercial rights; the free plan is non-commercial with
  attribution ([billing](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/administration/billing.md)).
- ANEC (help article read through a search summary): attribution names ElevenLabs with
  "elevenlabs.io" or "11.ai". GAP: whether free-plan audio gains rights after upgrading; assume not.
