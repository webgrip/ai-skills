# Developer social content: evidence

Labels: **OFF** official platform or regulator · **DATA** large sample, often vendor-run ·
**PRAC** practitioner · **GAP** not verified. Topics without evidence are listed at the end; do not
state rules about them as fact.

## Developer trust

- DATA: Stack Overflow 2025 developer survey (49,009 respondents): 46 % distrust the accuracy of AI
  tools against 33 % who trust it; favourable views 60 %, down from 72 % in 2024; the top
  frustration, named by 66 %, is "almost right, but not quite"
  ([summary](https://shiftmag.dev/stack-overflow-survey-2025-ai-5653/)).
- PRAC: show the working tool and avoid "revolutionary"
  ([okoone](https://www.okoone.com/spark/marketing-growth/why-software-developers-dont-buy-the-hype/));
  64 % of developer-marketing leads rate community and events as the highest return
  ([draft.dev 2026](https://draft.dev/2026-developer-marketing-survey)).

## LinkedIn

The mechanics live in the `linkedin-post` skill. Summary: external links cost reach (size disputed,
roughly 19 to 27 % in practitioner datasets), personal profiles out-reach company pages, comments
and dwell time drive distribution.

## YouTube Shorts

- OFF: the "stayed to watch vs swiped away" metric; every start counts as a view since 31 March 2025.
- PRAC: a 70 % swipe-away threshold for a broken hook is a vendor heuristic, not YouTube's
  ([vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-algorithm/)).

## Hacker News

- OFF: Show HN is for something people can run or try, ideally without signing up; landing pages,
  waitlists, blog posts and videos do not qualify; the author takes part in the thread
  ([showhn](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html)).
- OFF: no voting rings; plain titles ([guidelines](https://news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html)).
- PRAC: honest trade-offs, why you built it, the author answering for the first hours
  ([write-up](https://lucasfcosta.com/hn-launch)).
- GAP: Show HN volume rose sharply with AI tools and new accounts face posting limits.

## Reddit

- PRAC: the "10 % self-promotion" norm is etiquette, not a site rule; each subreddit sets its own
  rules in its sidebar ([summary](https://redship.io/blog/reddit-self-promotion-rules)).
- GAP: r/programming is effectively comment-only for your own projects; r/selfhosted is friendlier
  to self-hostable open projects.

## Legal and disclosure

- OFF: YouTube asks for disclosure of realistic depictions of real people, events or places only
  ([14328491](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491)).
- PRAC: TikTok requires labels on realistic AI content; Meta labels content from provenance
  metadata such as C2PA; keep that metadata intact
  ([summary](https://metricool.com/how-to-label-ai-content/)).
- PRAC: the EU AI Act's Article 50 transparency duties apply from 2 August 2026; a clearly stylised
  explainer with a synthetic narrator is probably low risk; confirm with counsel
  ([Baker McKenzie](https://connectontech.bakermckenzie.com/new-eu-guidance-on-ai-transparency-what-should-companies-be-doing-from-2-august-2026)).
- OFF: Atlassian allows its marks only to refer to its products or show compatibility, unmodified,
  from official sources, never combined with your own mark
  ([trademark](https://www.atlassian.com/legal/trademark)). GAP: GitHub and GitLab brand terms.
- OFF: ElevenLabs paid plans grant commercial rights; free-plan output is non-commercial with
  attribution.

## Not evidenced

posting cadence, share-versus-save ratios, first-hour rules, loop bonuses on Shorts.
