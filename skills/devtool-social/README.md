# devtool-social

Social content for a founder-led developer tool that has to reach sceptical developers and the
people who buy for them.

The skill assigns each platform a job (discovery, explanation, trust, conversation), turns long
audience videos into vertical Shorts, orders a launch, and defines what counts as working. It leans
on what is evidenced (developer distrust of AI hype, Hacker News and Reddit rules, YouTube's Shorts
metrics) and marks the rest, including X, TikTok, posting times and cadence, as tests to run.

Example prompts: "how do we go viral with these videos?", "turn this explainer into Shorts",
"write a hook for our launch".

Pairs with `linkedin-post`, `humanize`, `youtube-upload-kit` and `seekframe`.
