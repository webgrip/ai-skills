# What the consolidation added

The English catalog holds 206 entries that apply to English prose. 38 were already covered by the house copy rule or the blog-writer de-AI-ify pass; 168 were not. This file lists the additions so the gain is visible without reading the whole catalog. Entries scoped to one language are left out here and held in the catalog data.

## Sentence constructions (17)

- **Agentless passive and subjectless fragment** `agentless-passive-subjectless-fragment` — The actor is hidden or the subject dropped in the clipped shape models use to compress feature descriptions: agentless passives ('The results are preserved automatically.', 'Support for nested queries was added.') and verbless or subjectless fragments ('No configuration file needed.'). Severity context; from humanizer, no-ai-slop, avoid-ai-writing-skill-a, avoid-ai-writing-skill-b and others.
- **Cleft and dummy-noun emphasis (what matters is, the point is that, which is to say)** `cleft-dummy-noun` — A claim is foregrounded through a cleft or a dummy noun instead of being stated: 'What matters is X', 'What's important here is', 'The key is that', Korean '주목할 점은' (the point to note is), 'X은 ~라는 점에 있다' (X lies in the point that), '필요한/중요한 것은 X이다', '문제는 / 핵심은 / 관건은'; the wrap-up ending '~다는 것이다 / ~다는 뜻이다' (the thing is that / which is to say); the bound nouns 점·바·수·데 ('나아갈 바는', '할 수가 있다', '하는 데에'); and the inverted this-is-why closer '~하는 이유다'. Severity cluster; from im-not-ai-taxonomy, im-not-ai-method.
- **False range (from X to Y)** `false-range` — 'From X to Y' is used where X and Y are not endpoints of any real scale, so the phrase implies a span with a meaningful middle that does not exist ('from the Big Bang to dark matter', 'from ancient civilizations to modern startups', 'From innovation to implementation to cultural transformation'). Severity always; from humanizer, avoid-ai-writing-skill-a, avoid-ai-writing-detector, tropes-fyi and others.
- **Hypotactic smoothness (suspiciously clean grammar)** `hypotactic-smoothness` — Every sentence resolves grammatically: no fragments, no sentences starting with 'And' or 'But', no comma splices for effect, no anacoluthon or broken-off constructions, and no occasional wordy phrasing ('as a result of', 'in order to', 'the fact that'), which are the human fingerprints that models sand away. Severity context; from avoid-ai-writing-skill-a, vollmer, wikipedia-c.
- **Invented contrast-pair mirroring** `invented-contrast-pair` — A contrast is forced into symmetry where one half is a legitimate term of art and the other a phantom counterpart invented to balance the sentence ('false precision rather than genuine accuracy'). Severity context; from avoid-ai-writing-skill-b, avoid-ai-writing-detector.
- **Confidence by litotes** `litotes-confidence` — A claim is made by negating its opposite ('not difficult', 'not optional') so that it is presupposed rather than asserted or argued, which puts it beyond questioning. Severity context; from velitchkov.
- **Mirrored-clause symmetry and same-skeleton sentence runs** `mirrored-clause-symmetry` — Two or more clauses or sentences are built on one grammatical frame with only a couple of slots swapped, set side by side to convey contrast or a second case: 'High-metadiscourse prose runs the channel at high redundancy; thinned prose runs it at high density', 'A cart is an object in the system. Severity cluster; from avoid-ai-writing-skill-b, velitchkov.
- **Modality flattening (every hedge rendered as ~수 있다 / can)** `modal-flattening` — Every shade of epistemic uncertainty ('might', 'may well', 'could possibly', 'there is a chance') is rendered with the same modal, Korean ~수 있다, so the modality of a text is monotone. Severity cluster; from im-not-ai-taxonomy.
- **Nominalization and verb periphrasis (made a decision, has the ability to, N 능력)** `nominalization-inflation` — A single direct verb or adjective is replaced by a verb plus abstract noun or a modal periphrasis: 'made a decision' for 'decided', 'has the ability to' for 'can', 'the implementation of' for 'implementing', 'X capability' or 'ability to X' rendered as noun + 능력 in Korean. Severity cluster; from no-ai-slop, im-not-ai-taxonomy, im-not-ai-method.
- **Orphaned demonstrative and bad-subject sentence** `orphaned-demonstrative` — 'This', 'that' or 'these' opens a sentence as its subject with no clear referent, typically 'This highlights ...' or 'This underscores ...' after a paragraph of mixed claims; more generally the grammatical subject is not the topic of the sentence and the real agent or idea sits in an object or modifier ('Readers are better guided when the subject matches the main idea'). Severity cluster; from vollmer, im-not-ai-method.
- **Participial tail (superficial -ing analysis)** `participial-tail` — A trailing present-participle clause is bolted onto a plain fact to supply an interpretation the source does not provide: significance ('underscoring the importance of', 'highlighting the need for'), a trend ('reflecting a broader trend toward', 'marking a significant shift in'), legacy or symbolism ('symbolizing', 'showcasing', 'contributing to the region's rich cultural heritage'), or a purpose ('ensuring', 'fostering'). Severity always; from humanizer, no-ai-slop, avoid-ai-writing-skill-a, wikipedia-a and others.
- **Agentless prescription (must be addressed, X is needed)** `prescriptive-agentless-necessity` — Deontic statements pile up with no actor named: Korean ~해야 한다 (must) / ~할 필요가 있다 (there is a need to) five or more times, and the abstract-noun necessity '혁신이 필요하다' (innovation is needed), '변화가 필요하다' (change is needed). Severity cluster; from im-not-ai-taxonomy, im-not-ai-method.
- **Rhetorical self-question (The result? Devastating.)** `rhetorical-self-question` — The writer poses a question to themself and answers it, or fires questions as a stalling device. Severity always; from humanizer, avoid-ai-writing-skill-b, avoid-ai-writing-detector, wikipedia-b and others.
- **Same-opener sentence runs (anaphora)** `same-opener-runs` — Three or more consecutive sentences open on the same word or words ('They assume that . Severity context; from humanizer, avoid-ai-writing-skill-b, avoid-ai-writing-detector, tropes-fyi.
- **Stranded auxiliary contrast (the data didn't.)** `stranded-auxiliary-contrast` — A reversal lands on a bare auxiliary: 'The tool died; the data didn't.', 'Reading mostly passed. Severity cluster; from avoid-ai-writing-skill-b, avoid-ai-writing-detector.
- **Synonym doubling (role and function, new and innovative)** `synonym-doubling` — Two near-synonymous modifiers or abstract nouns are coordinated on one head so the phrase says the same thing twice: Korean '중요하고 핵심적인 역할' (important and core role), '새롭고 혁신적인 접근' (new and innovative approach), '지속적이고 꾸준한 노력' (continuous and steady effort), and the noun-pair variant '~로서의 역할과 기능' (role and function as ...), '~의 의미와 가치' (meaning and value of ...). Severity cluster; from im-not-ai-taxonomy, im-not-ai-method.
- **Tailing negation fragment and deflating tail clause** `tailing-negation` — A sentence ends with a comma and a terse negative tail in place of a full clause. Severity cluster; from humanizer, avoid-ai-writing-skill-a, velitchkov.

## Rhetorical moves and tone (28)

- **Announced interest and claimed emotion** `announced-interest` — A cue pre-interprets importance or claims a reaction the writing has not earned: 'Here's what's interesting', 'Here's the interesting part', 'What surprised me most', 'What struck me was', 'I was fascinated to discover', 'The most interesting part', and the bare section-header form ('Interesting aspect:'). Severity always; from avoid-ai-writing-skill-a, avoid-ai-writing-skill-b, avoid-ai-writing-detector.
- **Reader-directed closer (endorsement, CTA, obligation)** `call-to-action-closer` — A closing line instructs the reader instead of saying anything: the social endorsement sign-off ('This one is worth your time:', 'Bookmark this.', 'Do yourself a favor and read this.', 'Thank me later.'), the landing-page invitation ('Let's Connect!', 'Drop me a message', 'I'm always happy to hear from fellow Wikimedians!'), and the policy-report obligation ending where paragraph after paragraph closes on 'there is a need to' (Korean ~할 필요가 있다). Severity cluster; from avoid-ai-writing-skill-b, avoid-ai-writing-detector, wikipedia-b, im-not-ai-method.
- **Clean-consequence connector (unearned inevitability)** `clean-consequence-connector` — A connector asserts that the conclusion follows automatically from what came before, lending inevitability the argument has not earned: 'comes straight out of this', 'falls out of', 'follows directly', and the paragraph closer that declares a consequence with no path (Korean 결국 ~로 이어진다, '(ultimately) leads to'). Severity cluster; from velitchkov, im-not-ai-method.
- **Deeper-truth framing and the reframe** `deeper-truth-framing` — A stock phrase presents an ordinary point as a hidden or fundamental insight, or asserts that the matter is obvious instead of showing it: 'the real question is', 'at its core', 'what really matters', 'the heart of the matter', 'make no mistake', 'the truth is', 'The reality is simpler', 'History is unambiguous on this point', and the reveal that waves away everything said so far ('but none of them is the real story. Severity cluster; from humanizer, avoid-ai-writing-skill-b, tropes-fyi, velitchkov.
- **Didactic disclaimers and unsolicited caveats** `didactic-disclaimers` — Advice-to-the-reader disclaimers in text that should simply state facts: 'it's important to note', 'it's crucial to remember', 'always check before you use something', jurisdiction-varies warnings ('may vary'), 'to prevent confusion' disambiguation, and the closing recommendation that the reader go consult a source themselves ('For deeper insights, listening to tracks on platforms like Spotify or Deezer is recommended, as lyrics and production details aren't fully documented'). Severity context; from wikipedia-c, willfrancis, wikipedia-b.
- **Fake-casual register (props instead of a voice)** `fake-casual-register` — The costume a model puts on when asked for a lowercase-casual social voice: a kit of props that outsource drama to the prop instead of the content. Severity cluster; from avoid-ai-writing-skill-a, avoid-ai-writing-skill-b, avoid-ai-writing-detector.
- **False profundity (insight shape, no claim)** `false-profundity-truism` — A sentence carries the shape of insight, a contrast, a reversal or an epigram, but contains nothing a reader could act on or disagree with: 'Everyone has moments of doubt.', 'Change is the only constant.', 'At the end of the day, we are all human.' These are the safest possible sentences, true of everyone and so of no one. Severity always; from vollmer, willfrancis, hassid.
- **Flat affect (one tone, no engagement)** `flat-affect` — A single tone is held for a whole piece with no drift from analytical to angry to tender, the cadence never varies, and the interactional markers of an argumentative voice thin out: fewer questions, fewer asides, less direct address to the reader. Severity cluster; from vollmer.
- **Generic conclusion and future-narrative closer** `generic-conclusion` — A closing line gestures at the future or at certainty without a claim anyone could check: 'The future looks bright', 'Only time will tell', 'One thing is certain', 'As we move forward'. Severity always; from avoid-ai-writing-skill-a, avoid-ai-writing-skill-b, avoid-ai-writing-detector, im-not-ai-method and others.
- **Historical analogy stacking** `historical-analogy-stacking` — A rapid-fire list of past technologies, companies or revolutions is invoked to borrow their weight: 'like the printing press, the telegraph, and the internet before it', 'Apple didn't build Uber. Severity cluster; from avoid-ai-writing-skill-a, tropes-fyi.
- **Importance labelling (telling the reader what weight to give)** `importance-labelling` — An adjective, superlative or gloss asserts that something is important, crucial, significant, surprising, subtle or obvious in place of showing why: 'plays a crucial role in', 'it cannot be overstated', 'one of the most important', 'this represents a broader shift', 'the decision symbolizes a commitment to excellence', 'it speaks to a larger trend in the industry'. Severity always; from willfrancis, no-ai-slop, avoid-ai-writing-skill-a, im-not-ai-method and others.
- **Invented crowd contrast (forced contrarianism)** `invented-crowd-contrast` — A claim is propped on an implied lagging crowd nobody named: 'Everyone says X, but they're wrong', 'the conventional wisdom is backwards', and the date-stamped trailing clause 'while everyone else was still debating timelines', 'while the industry wrote thinkpieces', 'while everyone else played catch-up'. Severity context; from avoid-ai-writing-skill-b, avoid-ai-writing-detector.
- **Launch-copy dramatic introduction (Meet X, your new favorite Y)** `launch-copy-introduction` — A product is introduced like a game-show contestant instead of described: 'Enter Flowdesk.', 'Meet Flowdesk, your new favorite treasury dashboard', 'Say hello to Flowdesk', 'Think Notion meets Figma'. Severity context; from avoid-ai-writing-skill-a, avoid-ai-writing-skill-b, avoid-ai-writing-detector.
- **Lingering-attention claim (the line I keep coming back to)** `lingering-attention-claim` — A share post opens by claiming the thing has occupied the writer's mind for a duration, before the reader has any reason to care: 'the line I keep coming back to', 'I can't stop thinking about this', 'still thinking about this one', 'this has been rattling around in my head all week'. Severity context; from avoid-ai-writing-skill-b, avoid-ai-writing-detector.
- **Metaphor overuse (dead metaphor, generative metaphor)** `metaphor-overuse` — One figure is introduced and then never dropped: the same figurative noun recurs five to ten times across a piece ('the ecosystem needs ecosystems to build ecosystem value', 'walls and doors' thirty times, 'primitives' in every paragraph), where a human writer would use it once and move on. Severity cluster; from tropes-fyi, im-not-ai-method.
- **Narrated candor and performed vulnerability** `narrated-candor` — Announcing one's own disclosure instead of disclosing, or performing self-awareness that costs nothing: 'I want to be upfront:', 'To be fully transparent:', 'in the interest of full disclosure', 'Two caveats I would rather flag than let you discover later:', 'And yes, I'm openly in love with the platform model'. Severity cluster; from avoid-ai-writing-skill-b, avoid-ai-writing-detector, tropes-fyi.
- **Patronizing analogy (Think of it as, less a hammer more a scalpel)** `patronizing-analogy` — An unrequested analogy is offered in place of the claim or the instruction: 'Think of it as a Swiss Army knife for your workflow', 'Think of it like a highway system for data', 'It's like asking someone to buy a car they are only allowed to sit in', and the paired-image form 'Less a hammer, more a scalpel', which hands the reader two pictures and no advice. Severity context; from tropes-fyi, hassid.
- **Performed-insight phrase** `performed-insight-phrase` — Essayist tics that announce profundity instead of delivering it: 'sit with that for a moment', 'that's not nothing', 'you already know the answer', 'the punchline is', 'don't take my word for it', 'that's the whole point', 'that's the part nobody mentions', 'the only metric that matters', 'X is dead; long live X', sentence-initial 'Turns out'. Severity cluster; from avoid-ai-writing-skill-b, avoid-ai-writing-detector.
- **Phantom rebuttal, straw option and corrective pivot** `phantom-rebuttal` — The text argues with an interlocutor it invented. Severity cluster; from humanizer, velitchkov.
- **Reflexive AI-humility move** `reflexive-ai-humility` — The writer flags that it is itself a language model, or that its own claims may be wrong, as a gesture of modesty rather than as a limit on a specific claim: 'this essay is written by one of the systems under examination', 'Most of the above is structured impression, and could be wrong'. Severity cluster; from velitchkov.
- **Register mismatch (press-release voice by default)** `register-mismatch` — The register is chosen by habit rather than by context: a polished corporate answer to a casual question, sentences that read like a press release when spoken aloud, and the friendly-helpful conversational default that reinforcement training rewards. Severity context; from willfrancis, goedecke.
- **Review-process deflection (defending contested text)** `review-process-deflection` — When the provenance or quality of a contribution is questioned, the reply argues about the process instead of the text: asking the critic to identify precisely what is wrong ('please point out exactly which parts', 'let me know which parts'), dismissing the concern as unproven ('unsubstantiated speculation', 'without explaining how they are substantively flawed'), redirecting to collaboration ('let's focus on improving the content', 'Let's work together to strengthen the article rather than engage in unnecessary conflict'), and announcing that an edit addressed reviewer feedback ('Addressed reviewer feedback by improving sourcing, formatting, and neutrality'). Severity cluster; from wikipedia-c.
- **Scarcity-of-knowledge claim (nobody talks about this)** `scarcity-of-knowledge-claim` — The text claims that its point is unnoticed, hidden or suppressed, where no such scarcity exists: 'what nobody tells you about', 'the failure mode nobody's naming', 'a problem nobody talks about', 'the insight everyone's missing', 'This is the part most people skip', 'What most people get wrong'. Severity always; from avoid-ai-writing-skill-a, avoid-ai-writing-detector, no-ai-slop.
- **Significance signaling (framing before the content lands)** `significance-signaling` — A clause tells the reader that what follows is significant, or how to read it, instead of letting it be: 'This matters because', 'That reduction is useful, because', 'That last part matters more than it sounds', 'The key point is', 'As you can see', 'In other words' restating what was just said. Severity cluster; from velitchkov, no-ai-slop.
- **Speculative scenario opener (Imagine a world where)** `speculative-scenario-opener` — An argument opens with a hypothetical world that lists desirable outcomes instead of making a claim: 'Imagine a world where every deploy is instant', 'Picture a future in which', 'Envision a world where'. Severity cluster; from avoid-ai-writing-skill-a, avoid-ai-writing-detector, tropes-fyi.
- **Stacked hedges on one claim** `stacked-hedges` — Two or more qualifiers piled on a single predicate until it says nothing: 'could potentially possibly be argued that . Severity always; from humanizer, avoid-ai-writing-skill-b, im-not-ai-taxonomy, im-not-ai-method and others.
- **Stakes inflation (world-historical framing of an ordinary subject)** `stakes-inflation` — The importance of the subject or of what is about to be said is raised beyond anything the content supports: 'This will fundamentally reshape how we think about everything', 'will define the next era', 'the stakes have never been higher', 'now more than ever', 'because they shape everything that follows'. Severity always; from tropes-fyi, avoid-ai-writing-skill-b, velitchkov.
- **Sycophancy (validating the reader before answering)** `sycophancy` — Praise of the requester, their question or their feelings precedes the answer: 'Great question!', 'You're absolutely right', 'That's an excellent point', 'What a thoughtful observation'. Severity always; from humanizer, avoid-ai-writing-skill-b, avoid-ai-writing-detector, vollmer and others.

## Paragraph and document structure (21)

- **Bullet lists of bare noun phrases** `bare-noun-phrase-bullets` — Five or more consecutive bullet items that are each a short (six words or fewer) adjective-plus-noun phrase with no finite verb, all the same grammatical shape and length, none asserting anything checkable ('Stable mining efficiency', 'Reliable pool connectivity'). Severity cluster; from avoid-ai-writing-skill-a, avoid-ai-writing-skill-b, avoid-ai-writing-detector.
- **Bullets where prose belongs** `bullets-instead-of-prose` — Bulleted, especially nested, lists imposed on ideas that are connected or need context and would read better as a paragraph: eight or more bullets in under 200 words, three or more bullet blocks in a row in an essay, column or report, or any bullet list where two sentences of prose would do. Severity cluster; from no-ai-slop, avoid-ai-writing-skill-a, avoid-ai-writing-skill-b, im-not-ai-taxonomy and others.
- **Canned profile page** `canned-profile-page` — A profile or bio page following a fixed template: headings like 'Welcome To My User Page!', 'About Me', 'My Interests', 'My Contributions', 'Let's Connect!', inline-header bullets with an emoji per item, bold or Markdown-bold counts ('20+ articles'), and a cheerful sign-off ('Happy Editing! 🚀'). Severity cluster; from wikipedia-c.
- **Circular return ending** `circular-return-ending` — A closing sentence that loops back to the opening image or question ('And so we return to where we began') as a substitute for an ending that lands on something new. Severity cluster; from tropes-fyi.
- **Colon-subtitle headings (X: Y)** `colon-subtitle-headings` — Every heading takes the 'Noun: subtitle' shape, or its transformation variant 'X: from A to B' ('X: A에서 B로'). Severity cluster; from im-not-ai-taxonomy, im-not-ai-method.
- **Concede-salvage-rebut reply template** `concede-salvage-rebut-template` — When a user points to an error and the model agrees, the first paragraph of the reply follows a fixed three-beat shape: admit the user is right, point out what is still valid, then close with a variant of 'But that's not all it's doing, and the surplus is what you're pointing at'. Severity cluster; from velitchkov.
- **Despite-its-challenges outlook formula** `despite-challenges-outlook` — A stock closing block, often under a heading like 'Challenges and Future Directions' or 'Future Outlook', shaped as two beats: 'Despite its [positive words], [subject] faces challenges...' followed by 'Despite these challenges, [it] continues to thrive' or speculation about initiatives that could benefit the subject. Severity always; from humanizer, wikipedia-a, tropes-fyi.
- **Headings that only contain other headings** `empty-parent-headings` — A heading whose entire content is lower-level headings, with no prose of its own: an outline node rendered as a section ('= Main Characters =' followed directly by '== Pixy =='). Severity cluster; from wikipedia-b.
- **Formulaic section headers (slot names and study-guide titles)** `formulaic-section-headers` — Headings that name a template slot rather than the content: 'Introduction', 'Overview', 'Body', 'Summary', 'Conclusion' (Korean 도입 / 본론 / 결론), slide-deck and study-guide titles ('Key Takeaways', 'Key Points', 'Key highlights', 'Key Statistics', 'Key Figures', 'Questions to Consider'), and the default 'X and Y' encyclopaedic headers, of which 'Awards and recognition' is nearly ubiquitous in generated articles. Severity cluster; from avoid-ai-writing-skill-b, im-not-ai-taxonomy, im-not-ai-method, wikipedia-b and others.
- **Fractal summaries (section preview and recap)** `fractal-summaries` — 'Tell them what you will tell them, tell them, tell them what you told them' applied at every level: a preview sentence under each heading ('In this section, we'll explore...', '이 섹션에서는 ~를 다룬다'), a mini-summary closing every section ('In summary', 'as we've seen in this section'), and sections that open by recapping the previous one. Severity cluster; from tropes-fyi, vollmer, im-not-ai-taxonomy, im-not-ai-method and others.
- **Headers over short text** `headers-over-short-text` — Section headings imposed on text too short to need navigation: a heading over a section of two sentences or fewer, more than three headings in under 300 words, H2/H3s in a short piece, or a discussion reply or comment split into titled sections (Markdown, plain-text titles or wiki subheadings, sometimes emoji-decorated). Severity cluster; from no-ai-slop, avoid-ai-writing-skill-b, vollmer, wikipedia-c.
- **Heading restated in the first line** `heading-restated-below` — A heading is followed by a one-line paragraph that only restates it ('## Performance' then 'Speed matters.') before the real content begins. Severity always; from humanizer, avoid-ai-writing-skill-b.
- **Listicle in prose (ordinal scaffolding)** `listicle-in-prose` — Numbered points disguised as running prose: consecutive paragraphs or sentences each opened with an ordinal ('The first wall is. Severity cluster; from tropes-fyi, im-not-ai-taxonomy, im-not-ai-method.
- **Narrative defaults (POV lock, flat pacing, tidy resolution)** `narrative-flatness` — Fiction that picks one narrative lane and stays in it with unnatural consistency (no drift between free indirect style, interior monologue and exterior scene), unfolds every scene at the same rate (no elision, selective summary or acceleration), and ties every ending up because the model does not trust the reader with ambiguity unless told to. Severity cluster; from vollmer.
- **Numbered list inflation** `numbered-list-inflation` — A count-headlined list ('Three key takeaways', 'Five things to know', 'Here are the top seven') chosen because numbered lists are structurally safe, then padded to reach the number rather than reflecting that many discrete parallel items. Severity context; from avoid-ai-writing-skill-b.
- **Paragraph-reshuffle immunity (missing bridges)** `paragraph-reshuffle-immunity` — Body paragraphs that can be swapped without the reader noticing: each is a self-contained module with no load-bearing link to its neighbours, so the piece is a list of points rather than an argument that builds. Severity context; from avoid-ai-writing-skill-a, avoid-ai-writing-skill-b.
- **Repeated sentence and paragraph shapes (robotic symmetry)** `repeated-sentence-shapes` — Mechanical symmetry beyond length: the same sentence construction recurring, identical paragraph templates (same order of moves, same closing sentence) within a piece or across unrelated pieces, every paragraph carrying the same internal cadence, punctuation density flat from paragraph to paragraph, and every sentence complete, balanced and tidy with no roughness or aside. Severity cluster; from no-ai-slop, avoid-ai-writing-detector, hassid, vollmer.
- **Title heading repeated above the content** `title-heading-duplicate` — The output opens with a level-1 heading (often bold or linked) that repeats the document or article title before any body text, because the model does not assume the surface already renders the title. Severity context; from wikipedia-a, wikipedia-b.
- **Topic sentence opening every paragraph** `topic-sentence-every-paragraph` — Every paragraph opens with a thesis-style topic sentence that summarises the paragraph, the English composition-textbook shape imported wholesale. Severity cluster; from im-not-ai-taxonomy, im-not-ai-method.
- **Verse form defaults (poetry)** `verse-form-defaults` — Poetry that falls into the model's defaults regardless of the request: rhyme where none was asked for, four-line stanzas across forms, iambic meter by default, and superficial fidelity to a form (a 14-line 'sonnet', a 19-line 'villanelle', a 39-line 'sestina' that honours the line count but not the internal rule). Severity cluster; from vollmer.
- **Wall-of-text replies** `wall-of-text-replies` — In conversational registers (issue and PR comments, chat, DMs, casual email), a reply-length text of roughly under 150 words with four or more sentences delivered as one unbroken paragraph. Severity context; from avoid-ai-writing-skill-b, avoid-ai-writing-detector.

## Punctuation and formatting (21)

- **Boldface density** `bold-overuse` — Boldface applied mechanically instead of marking one real emphasis point: every term or acronym in a sentence, a bolded key phrase in every sentence, every instance of a chosen word, 'key takeaways' bold on ordinary noun phrases, or decorative bold sprinkled mid-sentence. Severity cluster; from humanizer, no-ai-slop, avoid-ai-writing-skill-a, avoid-ai-writing-skill-b and others.
- **Capital letter after a colon** `capitalized-after-colon` — The clause after a colon starts with a capital letter where grammar, a proper noun, a title or code does not require it ('Two options remain: We ship now'). Severity context; from no-ai-slop.
- **Comma density** `comma-density` — Document-level comma excess: the share of sentences containing at least one comma exceeds 50% against a human baseline of about 26 to 33%, and commas are inserted at every kind of syntactic boundary so the part-of-speech variety on either side of commas is far above the human norm (KatFish: human 24.4 vs AI 59.4). Severity cluster; from im-not-ai-taxonomy, im-not-ai-method.
- **Curly quotation marks on a straight-quote surface** `curly-quotes` — Typographic quotes (U+201C, U+201D, U+2018) and the right-single-quote apostrophe (U+2019) on a surface whose convention is straight quotes: code, commit messages, Markdown, Reddit, plain email. Severity cluster; from humanizer, avoid-ai-writing-skill-a, wikipedia-b, tropes-fyi and others.
- **Emoji as decoration** `emoji-decoration` — Emoji placed in front of headings, list items or bold labels as visual icons, one per heading or bullet and often thematically matched to the text ('🚀 **Launch Phase:**'), used as bullet markers or for emphasis, or a lone sparkle closing a paragraph. Severity always; from humanizer, no-ai-slop, avoid-ai-writing-skill-a, avoid-ai-writing-skill-b and others.
- **Hashtag stuffing** `hashtag-stuffing` — A long trailing block of hashtags on a short post (six or more, typically one project-specific tag plus broad category tags such as #AI #Innovation #Technology). Severity context; from avoid-ai-writing-skill-a, avoid-ai-writing-skill-b, avoid-ai-writing-detector.
- **Skipped heading levels** `heading-level-skipping` — Sections begin at the third heading level (### or ===) with no second-level heading above them, or any heading sits more than one level below its parent. Severity always; from wikipedia-b.
- **Immaculate typography in a rough register** `immaculate-typography` — Perfect spacing, punctuation and capitalization where humans type fast: issue and PR comments, chat, DMs, Reddit, quick email replies. Severity cluster; from avoid-ai-writing-skill-a, avoid-ai-writing-detector, vollmer.
- **Level-1 headings for body sections** `level-1-heading-overuse` — Level-1 headings (# or = H1 =) used for ordinary body sections on a surface that reserves level 1 for the page title (Wikipedia, most CMSs, HTML documents with a rendered title). Severity context; from wikipedia-b.
- **List-label periods** `list-label-periods` — In a bulleted list whose items lead with a short label, the label ends with a period and the gloss runs on as a separate sentence ('- **Intros.** Years of conferences and operator network.') where a person would use a colon. Severity always; from avoid-ai-writing-skill-a.
- **Parenthetical gloss overload** `parenthetical-gloss-overload` — Explanatory parentheses of the form '(this means ...)' or '(이는 ~을 의미한다)' tacked onto claim after claim in dense succession. Severity cluster; from im-not-ai-taxonomy, im-not-ai-method.
- **Parenthetical stage directions in dialogue** `parenthetical-stage-directions` — Dialogue tagged with a bracketed emotion before the line, script-style ('Bob: (defensive) Why should I?'), in prose that is not a screenplay. Severity always; from vollmer.
- **Redundant acronym expansion** `redundant-acronym-expansion` — Every acronym is spelled out in parentheses at first use even when the audience owns the term, often in the reversed 'KPIs (Key Performance Indicators)' order, wrapped in bold, and done for every term in the sentence. Severity context; from humanizer.
- **Scare-quote excess** `scare-quotes` — Quotation marks around ordinary words for emphasis or ironic distance ('a "seamless" experience'), five or more per document. Severity cluster; from im-not-ai-taxonomy, im-not-ai-method.
- **Semicolon and colon rate skew** `semicolon-colon-skew` — The semicolon and colon repertoire deviates from human prose in a direction that depends on the model generation: either the marks never appear and their work is done by commas and dashes, or (GPT-5, a brevity bias) semicolons splice independent clauses at a rate well above ordinary prose, replacing full stops and conjunctions. Severity cluster; from vollmer, goedecke.
- **Thematic breaks between every section** `thematic-breaks` — A horizontal rule (---- in wikitext, --- or *** in Markdown, <hr>) inserted before every section or sub-section, common in Markdown output. Severity cluster; from wikipedia-b.
- **Title case headings** `title-case-headings` — Every main word of a section heading is capitalized ('Strategic Negotiations And Global Partnerships'), sometimes conjunctions included, where the surface's house style, medium or language uses sentence case. Severity context; from humanizer, avoid-ai-writing-skill-a, avoid-ai-writing-skill-b, avoid-ai-writing-detector and others.
- **Arrows as prose connectives** `unicode-arrows` — Typographic arrows (→, ⇒) or their ASCII forms (->, =>) used in running prose where a writer would say 'leads to', 'then' or write a sentence ('Input → Processing → Output', 'better outcomes → higher engagement'). Severity context; from tropes-fyi, vollmer.
- **Math-bold letters and bullet glyphs** `unicode-math-bold-and-bullets` — Bold faked with Unicode Mathematical Alphanumeric Symbols (𝗯𝗼𝗹𝗱) instead of markup, and list items marked with the Unicode bullet character (•) on a surface that has real list syntax. Severity always; from vollmer.
- **Unnecessary hyphenation** `unnecessary-hyphenation` — Hyphens where English does not want them: stock corporate compounds ('data-driven', 'cross-functional', 'high-quality') kept hyphenated in predicate position ('the report is high-quality'); attributive-only forms used adverbially or as nouns ('in real-time', 'over the long-term', 'works out-of-the-box'); compounds whose standard form is one word ('code-base', 'data-set', 'time-frame', 'road-map'); open noun phrases welded with a hyphen ('research-impact aggregator', 'data-source strategy'); and several hyphenated modifiers stacked before one noun. Severity cluster; from humanizer, avoid-ai-writing-skill-a, avoid-ai-writing-skill-b, avoid-ai-writing-detector.
- **Unnecessary small tables** `unnecessary-tables` — Small tables (a handful of rows) holding facts that read better as a sentence or an infobox: a two-column Metric/Figure or Name/Designation table, or a Feature/A/B comparison with a 'Comparison of X and Y' or 'Key Statistics' caption. Severity context; from wikipedia-b.

## Content and evidence (25)

- **Absent human friction (asides, quotes, era markers)** `absent-human-friction` — Corroborating absences rather than phrases: the text never interrupts itself with a parenthetical self-correction or a real aside, never admits an unresolved mixed feeling, quotes nobody, and carries no era-bound slang, meme or in-joke, or carries one that is a year or more stale. Severity context; from humanizer, im-not-ai-method.
- **Cliche, purple prose, no subtext** `cliche-and-purple-prose` — Stock phrasing and over-ornamented description with nothing beneath the surface: everything the text means is stated on the page, no layer sits under the dialogue or the image, and the ornament substitutes for observation. Severity cluster; from vollmer.
- **Tenuous ecosystem, heritage and conservation padding** `ecosystem-heritage-padding` — Writing about a species, place or artefact is padded with generic ties to the wider ecosystem, environment or cultural heritage even where the tie is unsupported, and dwells on conservation status, threats and preservation effort where no assessment and no effort exist. Severity cluster; from wikipedia-a.
- **Empty caveat slot** `empty-caveat-slot` — A concession sentence that names no concession: an unnamed challenge paired with an unnamed response ('Despite challenges, the organisation continues to thrive', 'While facing headwinds, it remains resilient'), or a stand-alone 'limits remain' line after a run of positive claims. Severity always; from avoid-ai-writing-skill-a, im-not-ai-method, avoid-ai-writing-skill-b, avoid-ai-writing-detector.
- **Fabricated bylines and datelines** `fabricated-bylines-and-datelines` — Publishing metadata that does not correspond to anyone or anywhere: author bios and headshots for reporters who do not exist, and date or place lines that contradict the events described. Severity always; from vollmer.
- **Fabricated first-person experience** `fake-first-person` — First-person experience claims appear in prose that had no author behind them: 'I've seen this a hundred times', 'in my experience', 'I'll admit', or a lyric 'I' with no body, situation, history or place. Severity context; from avoid-ai-writing-skill-b, vollmer.
- **Flat dialogue and as-you-know-Bob exposition** `flat-dialogue-and-exposition` — Characters are interchangeable: no distinct idiolect, verbal tic, dialect or pattern of evasion separates one speaker from another, and speech carries information the speakers already share so the reader can hear it. Severity cluster; from vollmer.
- **Gratuitous universals and category errors** `gratuitous-universals-and-category-errors` — Authority borrowed from a scope the writer cannot check ('taught in every first-year biochemistry course', 'every engineer knows'), and moral or evaluative adjectives attached to things that cannot carry them, including assumptions and abstractions treated as if they had intentions or virtues. Severity context; from avoid-ai-writing-skill-a, avoid-ai-writing-detector.
- **Hallucinated or unverifiable citations** `hallucinated-citations` — References that cannot carry the claim: URLs to sites that never existed or 404 with no archive copy, ISBNs that fail their checksum, DOIs that do not resolve or resolve to an unrelated paper, real journals with invented volumes, pages or author lists, books cited with no page number or link, placeholder identifiers built from a tiny number (pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3), and invented rules, policies or shortcut links that say something other than claimed. Severity cluster; from wikipedia-c, vollmer.
- **Lost prior-thread context** `lost-thread-context` — A reply does not answer what the earlier message asked: it responds to the topic rather than the question, drops the specifics raised upthread, and reintroduces information already settled. Severity cluster; from vollmer.
- **Analysis misattributed to a named source** `misattributed-source-analysis` — A retrieval-augmented model attaches its own interpretive gloss to a real named source or citation, whether or not that source says anything of the kind: 'Roger Ebert highlighted the lasting influence', 'Fridrichova highlights that . Severity always; from wikipedia-a.
- **No concrete particular (portable sentence)** `missing-concrete-detail` — The text stays at the level of the generic and the widely applicable: no proper noun, no brand, no street, no month, no odd quote, nothing that could only be here. Severity always; from humanizer, no-ai-slop, vollmer.
- **Notability name-dropping and over-attribution** `notability-name-dropping` — Importance is argued by cataloguing where the subject appeared rather than what was said: a roll-call of prestigious outlets, a classification of those outlets ('independent coverage', 'regional media outlets', 'trade publications'), or a follower count. Severity cluster; from humanizer, avoid-ai-writing-skill-a, avoid-ai-writing-detector, wikipedia-a and others.
- **Mixed or subtly off metaphor** `off-metaphor` — Images in the right semantic ballpark with the wrong physics: figures that fall apart the moment you picture them, or two incompatible figures welded into one sentence. Severity always; from vollmer.
- **Mood-saturated setting (pathetic fallacy)** `pathetic-fallacy-mood-setting` — Weather, rooms and objects exist only to mirror a character's feeling: rain that matches grief, a room that is cold because the marriage is. Severity always; from vollmer.
- **Pro-authoritarian framing** `pro-authoritarian-bias` — On political topics, generated text leans toward state-media framing, criticises the governments of freer countries more readily than repressive ones, and sometimes declines on safety grounds where the safety concern runs one direction. Severity context; from wikipedia-c.
- **Promotional and brochure register** `promotional-language` — Prose meant to inform drifts into advertisement: evaluative adjectives, travel-guide framing, and press-release verbs applied to places, communities, people, companies or products. Severity always; from humanizer, avoid-ai-writing-skill-a, avoid-ai-writing-skill-b, avoid-ai-writing-detector and others.
- **Significance and legacy inflation** `significance-inflation` — An ordinary fact is framed as a turning point, a legacy, a symbol, or evidence of a broad trend, using a small recognisable set of significance verbs and nouns: 'marks a pivotal moment', 'stands as a testament', 'plays a vital role', 'underscores its significance', 'enduring legacy', 'evolving landscape'. Severity always; from humanizer, no-ai-slop, avoid-ai-writing-skill-a, avoid-ai-writing-skill-b and others.
- **Source-count and consensus inflation** `source-count-inflation` — Sources are cited, but their number or breadth is overstated: one person's view is presented as widely held, plural 'reviewers', 'scholars' or 'publications' stand for a single cited name, 'several sources' appears where two are listed, and 'such as' implies a longer list the sources give no sign exists. Severity context; from wikipedia-a, tropes-fyi.
- **Speculative gap-fill dressed as fact** `speculative-gap-fill` — Where the model found nothing it asserts that nothing exists ('details are not widely documented') and then fills the hole with hedged invention introduced by 'likely', 'may', 'appears to have'. Severity always; from humanizer, avoid-ai-writing-skill-a, wikipedia-b.
- **Sudden polish above the writer's baseline** `sudden-polish-jump` — Diction and syntax leap above the writer's demonstrated level inside one piece or between consecutive pieces: clause structures, vocabulary and punctuation that no earlier work by this author shows. Severity context; from vollmer.
- **Situating the subject in an unnamed debate** `unnamed-debate-situating` — The subject is said to have generated, sparked, shaped, or prompted debate, discussion, or reflection, or to raise questions, with no debate, participant, venue or date ever named. Severity cluster; from wikipedia-a.
- **Vague expression of connection** `vague-association` — A concrete relationship (was CEO of, taught at, composed for, received) is replaced by an unspecified link: 'associated with', 'in connection with', 'connected to', 'particularly associated'. Severity cluster; from wikipedia-a.
- **Vague attribution to unnamed authority** `vague-attribution` — A claim is credited to an authority the text never identifies: experts, studies, research, observers, critics, analysts, industry reports, scholarship. Severity always; from humanizer, no-ai-slop, avoid-ai-writing-skill-a, avoid-ai-writing-skill-b and others.
- **Estimated range standing in for a measurement** `vague-numeric-range` — A numeric range is given where a single observed number would exist if the writer had done the thing: 'takes 5 to 10 minutes', 'between 20 and 30 requests'. Severity context; from hassid.

- **Collaborative closer and offer of further help** `collaborative-closer` — The chat sign-off addressed to the requester survives into the text. Severity always; from humanizer, avoid-ai-writing-skill-a, avoid-ai-writing-skill-b, avoid-ai-writing-detector and others.

- **Instructions and notes to the person publishing the text** `instructions-to-the-handler` — Text addressed to whoever will paste, submit or review the document, published inside it. Severity always; from wikipedia-b, wikipedia-c.
- **Letter register on a surface that is not correspondence** `letter-register-on-non-correspondence` — Business-letter framing on a ticket, a talk page, a comment, a form or an article: 'Dear Editorial Team,', 'I hope this message finds you well', 'I am writing to request an edit', 'Thank you for your understanding and assistance in this matter', 'Best regards,'. Severity always; from wikipedia-b, wikipedia-c, vollmer.
- **Provenance signals outside the text** `out-of-text-provenance-signal` — Evidence about how the text arrived rather than how it reads: the whole document appearing as a single paste event in the editor's version history, and an author's sudden shift to flawless or formal prose against their own baseline, especially where that baseline predates late 2022. Severity context; from vollmer, wikipedia-c.
- **Outline plan published instead of the section** `outline-plan-left-in-body` — A sentence describing what a section would contain is emitted in place of the content: 'This section would speculate on potential developments and the changing landscape of global energy.' - **Placeholder value in a structured field** `placeholder-in-metadata-field` — A citation, infobox or front-matter field holding a stub instead of a value: 'url=URL', 'INSERT_SOURCE_URL_30', 'SOURCE_PUBLISHER', 'PASTE_SPOTIFY_TRACK_URL_HERE', date stubs such as '2025-XX-XX' or '2022-11-XX' (usually in access-date, sometimes in date, and they raise tool errors), and hidden comments that defer content in a field ('<!-- Add if available with citation -->', instructions to upload an image). Severity always; from wikipedia-b.
- **Reasoning scaffolding and disclosed inference method** `reasoning-chain-leak` — Chain-of-thought scaffolding published as prose: 'Let me think step by step', 'Breaking this down', 'To approach this systematically', 'Here's my thought process', 'First, let's consider', and numbered reasoning steps that read as an internal monologue rather than an argument for a reader. Severity always; from avoid-ai-writing-skill-b, avoid-ai-writing-detector, wikipedia-b.
- **Roleplay action markers** `roleplay-action-markers` — Asterisk-wrapped stage directions from chat roleplay left in prose: '*nods*', '*sighs*', '*sighs and looks away*', '*leans in*'. Severity cluster; from avoid-ai-writing-detector.
- **Self-certification of compliance** `self-certification-of-compliance` — The text asserts its own neutrality, sourcing or rule-conformance instead of demonstrating it: 'Written to Wikipedia Manual of Style. Severity cluster; from wikipedia-b, wikipedia-c.
- **Templated change note (commit message, PR description, edit summary)** `templated-change-note` — A change description written as exhaustive procedural prose rather than a terse human note. Severity cluster; from wikipedia-c.
- **Unfilled placeholder in the body** `unfilled-placeholder` — A fill-in-the-blank slot the writer was meant to replace, shipped as-is: bracketed nouns ('[Your Name]', '[Entertainer's Name]', '[Specific Topic]', '[link to source list]'), bracketed instructions ('[Describe the specific section that needs editing and provide clear reasons why]', '[INSERT SOURCE URL]'), parenthesised prompts ('(Add your channel URL here)', '(If available)') and template variables ('{client_name}'). Severity always; from avoid-ai-writing-skill-a, avoid-ai-writing-detector, wikipedia-b, vollmer.

## Translationese (7)

- **Agentive by-passive (~에 의해 / ~에 의하여 + passive verb)** `agentive-by-passive` — The English agentive passive with a named agent ('generated by AI', 'published by the ministry') rendered as X에 의해/에 의하여 plus a passive verb, or adnominally as X에 의한. Severity cluster; from im-not-ai-taxonomy, im-not-ai-method.
- **'Beyond mere X' escalation (단순한 X를 넘어 Y / more than just X)** `beyond-mere-escalation` — Mid-sentence escalation that upgrades a thing by dismissing a smaller version of it: '단순한 X를 넘어 Y', a calque of English 'beyond mere X' and 'more than just X'. Severity always; from im-not-ai-method.
- **Whole English sentences quoted with a translation alongside** `embedded-english-quotation` — A full English sentence is pasted verbatim into the body as a quotation and immediately followed by its translation, so the reader gets the same claim twice in two languages. Severity context; from im-not-ai-taxonomy, im-not-ai-method.
- **Literal light-verb constructions (have / make / take / give + noun)** `light-verb-literalism` — An English light-verb construction ('have strong competitiveness', 'make a decision', 'take action', 'give support') is carried over word for word as verb + noun, where the target language says the same thing with a single predicate or a double-subject construction. Severity always; from im-not-ai-taxonomy, im-not-ai-method.
- **English gloss in parentheses repeated on every occurrence** `parenthetical-english-gloss` — A term is followed by its English original in parentheses every time it appears, so 주권 AI(Sovereign AI) is followed later by 주권 AI(Sovereign AI) again rather than by the Korean term alone. Severity cluster; from im-not-ai-taxonomy, im-not-ai-method.
- **Interference index (composite metric over the A-class signals)** `translationese-interference-index` — A metric, not a sentence-level pattern: Toury's (1995) interference axis operationalised as nine measurable signals combined into one index: inanimate_subject_rate (abstract-subject-all-purpose-verb), by_passive_count and double_passive_count (the three passive entries), pronoun_density (pronoun-over-translation), deul_overuse_rate (mechanical-plural-marking, held), relative_clause_nesting (left-branching-modifier-stack), have_make_literal_count (light-verb-literalism), double_particle_count (stacked-particles) and progressive_aspect_rate (passive-progressive-calque). Severity context; from im-not-ai-taxonomy.
- **Untranslated English marketing buzzword in a non-English text** `untranslated-marketing-loanword` — English marketing adjectives and verbs (seamless, robust, leverage, scalable, synergy, holistic) dropped untranslated and unexplained into a target-language sentence, breaking its rhythm and reading as display. Severity cluster; from im-not-ai-taxonomy, im-not-ai-method.
